Frequently asked questions

Why doesn't my balance match my bank account?

Track IT is a manual budgeting ledger, not a live banking connection. Verify your opening balance, recorded income and expenses, and whether any transactions were entered twice.

What is “safe to spend”?

It's an estimate of what may remain after upcoming bills and planned savings. It isn't a guaranteed bank balance or financial advice.

Will recurring income and bills appear on the calendar?

Recurring schedules can create upcoming entries in Track IT's financial calendar. These are expected events and don't automatically count as confirmed bank transactions or move money.

How do I change an income or expense?

Go to Transactions, tap the entry, then choose the appropriate edit or delete option.

Can I import a bank statement?

Track IT supports certain CSV and text-based PDF layouts. Review the extracted details carefully before approving them. Some bank formats and scanned statements may not work.

Can Track IT scan receipts?

Track IT includes on-device receipt text recognition for selected pictures. Always check the merchant and amount before saving.

How do I export or delete my information?

Open Explore → About Track IT → Manage My Data. You can export transactions to CSV or delete locally stored financial records. Files you've already exported and previous device backups may remain outside Track IT's control.

Does Track IT connect to my bank or require an account?

No Track IT account or banking password is required for the described offline-first version. Imported files are selected by you; there is no live bank connection.
