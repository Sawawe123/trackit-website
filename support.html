Frequently asked questions

Why is my balance different from my bank's balance?Track IT keeps a budget ledger based on the information you enter or approve. It is not a live banking connection. Check your opening balance, missing transactions and duplicates.
What is safe to spend?It is an estimate after considering recorded money and unpaid upcoming commitments. It is not a guaranteed bank balance.
Do recurring payments move real money?No. Recurring schedules populate a financial calendar. Track IT does not transfer funds automatically.
How do I edit or delete a transaction?Open Transactions, select the entry, then use the edit or delete action.
How do I export or remove my data?Open Explore → About Track IT → Manage My Data. Data exported to other apps and existing backups are not erased automatically.
Can I import bank statements?Some CSV files and certain text-based PDFs are supported. You must review any extracted entries; not every bank's document format will work.
