Privacy Policy

This policy describes how Track IT handles financial information within the app, and what happens if you contact its publisher.

Effective date: 26 October 2026

1. Who operates Track IT?

Track IT is published by Aaron Thant.

For privacy enquiries, requests, corrections, or complaints, contact sawawebwe@gmail.com.

2. Information used by the app

Track IT is a personal budgeting app. You can enter an opening balance, income and expenses, dates, transaction descriptions, budgets, savings goals, recurring financial schedules and app preferences. You can also choose to import financial documents or images.

The described version stores approved budgeting records locally on your iPhone. It does not require you to create a Track IT account or give Track IT your banking passwords. It is not connected to live bank accounts.

3. Bank statement and receipt processing

When you choose to import a supported CSV or text-based PDF bank statement, Track IT reads the selected file on your device and displays suggested transactions for your review. You decide which entries to save. The app does not create a permanent library of your original imported statements.

For receipt images you select, Track IT uses Apple's on-device text recognition to suggest transaction details. You must check the results before saving them. The app is designed not to save the selected receipt photo as a permanent receipt library.

Import processing may be inaccurate or unsupported for some document layouts. Track IT does not delete the original documents you keep in Files, iCloud or Photos, and deleting app records will not remove those originals.

4. How financial information is used

Your budgeting information is used in the app to calculate financial summaries, category spending, recurring calendar entries, budgets, savings progress, estimated safe-to-spend amounts, reminders and financial forecasts. These calculations are budgeting estimates and not verified bank balances.

In the described version, categorisation uses local rules and receipt text recognition uses on-device technology; it does not send your bank statements to a cloud-based AI categorisation service.

5. Sharing, websites and support emails

Track IT does not operate a developer-hosted cloud account system for storing your budgeting ledger, and the described version does not include advertising or third-party analytics SDKs. Financial data is not intentionally uploaded to a Track IT publisher server as part of ordinary budgeting.

If you email for support, your email address and anything you choose to send will be handled through your email provider and the publisher's email provider. Please do not email full bank statements, account passwords or card details.

This website is hosted using GitHub Pages. Visiting it may involve technical information, such as your IP address, being processed by GitHub under its own privacy practices. Your device or Apple services may also process app data through device backups and diagnostics, depending on your settings.

Providers of email and website hosting services may process information outside Australia. You can read GitHub's privacy statement for its website practices.

6. Device permissions and security

Track IT may use system permissions for selected photos, notifications and device authentication (such as Face ID or passcode). You can manage relevant permissions in iPhone Settings.

The app uses iOS local storage and device data-protection features, with an optional app-lock setting. No application can guarantee absolute security. Exported files and device backups are protected according to where you store them, not necessarily by Track IT's app-lock setting.

7. Retention, access, correction and deletion

Locally stored transactions and other budgeting information ordinarily remain on your device until you edit or delete them, delete all local app data, or remove the app. Track IT provides controls to edit entries, export transaction data to CSV, and delete local financial records.

These actions do not automatically erase bank statements or receipts stored elsewhere, previously exported files, information shared with other apps, or historical Apple device backups.

If you email us, your message and contact details may be retained for as long as reasonably needed to respond, address the issue and meet any applicable legal requirements. To request access to, correction of, or deletion of information held through support correspondence, contact sawawebwe@gmail.com.

8. Questions and privacy complaints

Send privacy requests or complaints to sawawebwe@gmail.com. Explain your concern and how you would like us to help, without sending unnecessary financial details. We will consider the request and respond within a reasonable period consistent with applicable law.

Depending on the laws that apply to your circumstances, you may also be entitled to raise an unresolved privacy complaint with a regulator. For information about privacy complaints in Australia, see the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner.

9. International users

Privacy rights and obligations may differ depending on where you live and which laws apply to Track IT's activities. If you have a request about how your data is handled, please contact sawawebwe@gmail.com.

10. Updates to this policy

This policy may be updated when Track IT's features or data handling change. We will publish the revised policy on this website with a new effective date and take any additional notice or consent steps required by applicable law.
