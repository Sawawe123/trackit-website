2. Data you enter

Track IT lets you enter or import transaction descriptions, amounts, dates, income schedules, bills, budgets and savings goals. Financial information may be sensitive to you. The intended app design stores approved budgeting records locally on your device rather than in developer-hosted accounts.

No Track IT account or banking password is required.

3. Statement and receipt imports

If you choose to import a supported bank statement, Track IT processes the selected file on-device, presents potential transactions for review and saves only the entries you approve. The intended implementation does not permanently store its own copy of the imported original file. The original in Files or iCloud remains under your control.

Optional receipt recognition uses device-based text recognition. Extraction can make mistakes, so review every detail.
